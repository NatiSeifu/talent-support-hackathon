# Evidence-Source Semantics Matrix

> Status: Initial contract complete.

| Source family | Strongest legitimate contribution | Weakly suggests | Cannot establish alone | Important caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Identity and organization | Person/account linkage; role/team state at a time | Organizational context and availability | Expertise, work quality, ownership, risk | False identity merges fabricate evidence |
| Software development | Recorded implementation, review, and collaboration events | Exposure, implementation experience, technical judgment | Expertise, production use, formal ownership, criticality | Mechanical work and line counts are misleading |
| Work management | Intended work, assignment, workflow, requirements, links | Expected responsibility and domain exposure | Performed work, correctness, expertise, impact | Completion and assignment are workflow states |
| Knowledge artifacts | What explicit knowledge a revision stated | Documentation coverage and recorded familiarity | Correctness, freshness, usability, transfer | Existence and edit recency are insufficient |
| Production operations | Runtime events, actions, deployments, and attributed reasoning | Operational exposure and scoped practical capability | Expertise, causality, ownership, criticality | Participation, diagnosis, action, and recovery differ |
| Ownership and architecture | Declared systems, mappings, responsibility, and dependencies | Formal accountability and technical adjacency | Stewardship, expertise, runtime truth, impact | Declarations and maps may be stale |
| Business impact | Native criticality, commitments, consequence, and recovery declarations | Organizational importance and consequence context | Expertise, concentration, probability, final risk | Native scales must not be universalized |
| Communication and collaboration | Recorded questions, answers, referrals, and attributed statements | Perceived relevance and informal exposure | Correctness, expertise, learning, transfer | Private and verbal work may be invisible |
| Knowledge validation | Scoped self-report, attestation, observed performance, and transfer tests | Current capability and transferability | Universal expertise or durable success beyond scope | Criteria, assistance, evaluator, and realism matter |
| Candidate and successor | Candidate claims, corroboration, demonstrated scope, per-gap coverage | Potential coverage and remaining evidence needs | Best candidate, hiring verdict, guaranteed readiness | Resume claims and adjacent evidence are not capability |

## Cross-Source Claim Requirements

| Broader claim | Required evidence dimensions |
|---|---|
| Expertise | Relevant exposure plus evidence of judgment or demonstrated capability, with freshness and contradiction handling |
| Observed stewardship | Repeated substantive maintenance or operational care over time |
| Knowledge concentration | Person-capability evidence distribution plus observability limitations |
| Documentation gap | Defined requirement plus scoped artifact retrieval and content assessment |
| Transferability | Artifact or interview output plus successor usability or demonstration evidence |
| Business consequence | Business Impact evidence linked to the relevant capability |
| Knowledge-loss concern | Criticality, concentration, transferability, departure exposure, freshness, and observability |
| Candidate gap coverage | Requirement plus candidate claim/corroboration/demonstration and contradiction state |

## Retrieval Versus Inference Boundary

- **Retrieval correctness:** Did the system obtain the relevant source records?
- **Evidence construction:** Did it create faithful narrow propositions?
- **Inference correctness:** Did it combine propositions without overclaiming?
- **Risk classification:** Did it combine the required risk dimensions correctly?
- **Uncertainty calibration:** Did it contest, remain unresolved, or abstain when
  appropriate?
- **Interview utility:** Did a question target and reduce a real unresolved gap?
- **Candidate coverage:** Did the system characterize evidence for each requirement
  without ranking beyond the evidence?
