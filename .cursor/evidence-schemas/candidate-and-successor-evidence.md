# Candidate And Successor Evidence Schema

> Status: Initial model complete.

## Purpose

Candidate and successor evidence records what an internal successor or external
candidate claims, what can be corroborated, and what capability has been directly
demonstrated against a specific organizational need.

This family supports gap-coverage analysis by keeping separate:

- organizational capability requirements;
- candidate identity and source materials;
- candidate-authored claims;
- verified credentials or work evidence;
- internal evidence imported from other source families;
- demonstrated capability from Knowledge Validation;
- remaining missing or contradictory evidence;
- planning assumptions about availability and development support.

It does not establish that a candidate will succeed, should be hired, is the “best”
candidate, or will become ready within a predicted period.

## Governing Boundaries

```text
Organizational need:
An evidence-backed capability or knowledge requirement derived outside this source
family.

Candidate claim:
What the candidate or resume states.

Corroborated experience:
Independent evidence supports some or all of the claim.

Demonstrated capability:
A scoped Knowledge Validation event observed relevant performance.

Gap coverage:
An assessment of how available evidence relates to a specific organizational need.

Hiring or staffing decision:
A human organizational decision outside the evidence source schema.
```

Knowledge gaps are not generated by a particular agent role here. Assessment
exercises and observed performance belong to Knowledge Validation and are linked,
not duplicated. Staffing recommendations and candidate rankings are downstream
product outputs, not source-family records.

## Objects

1. Organizational capability requirement reference
2. Candidate or successor subject
3. Candidate source document
4. Candidate-authored experience claim
5. Credential or external corroboration
6. Internal successor evidence link
7. Capability evidence link
8. Gap-coverage assessment
9. Availability and development-plan assumption
10. Historical successor outcome link

## Retrieval

Evidence may be retrieved from:

- candidate resumes and applications;
- internal employee profiles and work history;
- approved work samples or portfolios;
- certification and credential records;
- structured interview records;
- public contribution records when legally and ethically approved;
- references or background-verification systems within authorized scope;
- internal source-family evidence;
- Knowledge Validation sessions and outcomes;
- synthetic JSON for the demo.

Retrieval must record consent, authorization, source dates, redactions, verification
scope, and whether candidate-provided information could be independently checked.
Protected characteristics and unrelated personal information must not be used.

## Organizational Capability Requirement Reference

This family consumes an evidence-backed requirement; it does not create the
underlying expertise or risk assessment.

```python
from datetime import datetime
from enum import Enum

from pydantic import BaseModel, Field


class CapabilityRequirementKind(str, Enum):
    SYSTEM_KNOWLEDGE = "system_knowledge"
    OPERATIONAL_PROCEDURE = "operational_procedure"
    DIAGNOSTIC_REASONING = "diagnostic_reasoning"
    IMPLEMENTATION_CAPABILITY = "implementation_capability"
    TECHNICAL_JUDGMENT = "technical_judgment"
    DECISION_RATIONALE = "decision_rationale"
    BUSINESS_PROCESS = "business_process"
    SECURITY_OR_COMPLIANCE = "security_or_compliance"
    OTHER = "other"
    UNKNOWN = "unknown"


class CapabilityRequirementRef(BaseModel):
    capability_requirement_id: str
    requirement_kind: CapabilityRequirementKind
    requirement_statement: str

    subject_system_id: str | None = None
    subject_component_id: str | None = None
    originating_assessment_ids: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    supporting_evidence_ids: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    contradicting_evidence_ids: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)

    resolution_status: str
    valid_at: datetime | None = None
    observability_limitations: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
```

The requirement must remain traceable to the evidence model. A generic job
description or unsupported manager preference must not silently become an
organizational knowledge gap.

## Gap-Coverage Assessment

Gap coverage is a downstream evidence assessment, not a source fact or candidate
score.

```python
class CoverageDisposition(str, Enum):
    DEMONSTRATED = "demonstrated"
    PARTIALLY_DEMONSTRATED = "partially_demonstrated"
    CORROBORATED_EXPERIENCE = "corroborated_experience"
    CLAIMED_ONLY = "claimed_only"
    ADJACENT_EVIDENCE = "adjacent_evidence"
    CONTRADICTED = "contradicted"
    INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE = "insufficient_evidence"
    NOT_ASSESSED = "not_assessed"
    ABSTAINED = "abstained"


class CandidateGapCoverageAssessment(BaseModel):
    gap_coverage_assessment_id: str
    candidate_subject_id: str
    capability_requirement_id: str
    disposition: CoverageDisposition

    assessment_statement: str
    supporting_evidence_ids: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    contradicting_evidence_ids: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    validation_link_ids: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    assumptions: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    limitations: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)

    assessed_at: datetime
    assessment_method: str
    assessment_version: str | None = None
```

Coverage assessments remain per requirement. They must not be collapsed into an
overall candidate ranking without a separately justified decision policy.

## Availability And Development-Plan Assumptions

Availability and proposed development support affect planning but do not prove
capability or determine a guaranteed ramp time.

```python
class CandidatePlanningAssumption(BaseModel):
    planning_assumption_id: str
    candidate_subject_id: str
    capability_requirement_ids: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)

    assumption_text: str
    source_record_ids: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    valid_from: datetime | None = None
    valid_until: datetime | None = None
    resolution_status: str
```

Examples include stated availability, departing-person overlap, dedicated learning
time, mentorship, documentation access, drills, and environment access.

The initial model defines no predicted number of ramp days or weeks. Future ramp
projections require longitudinal calibration against historical outcomes and must
preserve their assumptions and uncertainty.

## Supported Propositions

Candidate and successor records may support:

- a candidate submitted a particular document or claim;
- a credential, role, work product, or portion of a claim was corroborated;
- internal source-family evidence was relevant to a particular requirement;
- a Knowledge Validation event demonstrated or failed to demonstrate a scoped
  capability;
- evidence for a requirement was claimed, adjacent, corroborated, demonstrated,
  contradictory, missing, or unassessed;
- a planning assumption about availability or support was recorded;
- later work or operational evidence corroborated or contradicted an earlier
  coverage assessment.

## Cannot Establish

This source family cannot establish:

- that a resume or interview claim is true without corroboration;
- that general domain experience covers company-specific knowledge;
- that activity volume or proximity equals readiness;
- that adjacent experience will transfer;
- that a single exercise predicts durable job performance;
- that an internal candidate is willing, available, or entitled to a role;
- a guaranteed ramp time;
- a universally best candidate;
- a hiring, promotion, compensation, or staffing decision;
- that protected or unavailable personal information should influence assessment;
- causal success from later outcomes.

## Time, Privacy, And Fairness

- Preserve claim, credential, assessment, validation, and observation times.
- Do not project old experience onto current systems without support.
- Record consent, authorization, access limitations, and retention requirements.
- Exclude protected characteristics and unrelated personal information.
- Preserve accommodations and do not misclassify approved assistance as lack of
  capability.
- Distinguish missing evidence from negative evidence.
- Provide candidates or authorized reviewers a correction path for disputed source
  records where policy requires it.
- High-stakes decisions require human governance beyond this evidence model.

## Canonical Failure Cases

1. A resume claims ownership where the person was one contributor.
2. Employment verification confirms a title but not the claimed responsibilities.
3. Public work represents only a small and unrepresentative portion of experience.
4. An internal successor has many review events but only state-only approvals.
5. Incident participation is upgraded into independent diagnosis capability.
6. Adjacent system experience is mistaken for company-specific operational knowledge.
7. A candidate demonstrates one procedure and is assessed as covering the whole
   system.
8. An exercise result ignores material hints or unavailable normal tools.
9. A stale certification is treated as current demonstrated capability.
10. Missing public evidence is treated as lack of experience.
11. A planning assumption promises mentorship that is not actually available.
12. A predicted ramp time is presented without longitudinal calibration.
13. A candidate is ranked by an unjustified aggregate score.
14. A candidate-authored claim is treated as verified because it is detailed.
15. A later successful outcome is attributed solely to the original handoff.
16. Protected personal information leaks into gap-coverage reasoning.
17. A requirement itself is unsupported or outdated.
18. Two candidates are compared using materially different validation conditions.
19. A candidate correction is ignored after source data is shown to be wrong.
20. “Insufficient evidence” is interpreted as “not capable.”

## Accepted Decisions

1. Keep organizational requirements external and evidence-backed.
2. Do not encode agent consensus or a particular orchestration architecture as gap
   provenance.
3. Treat resumes and applications as candidate-authored claims.
4. Preserve verification scope; partial corroboration must not become full
   verification.
5. Reference internal source evidence without changing its original semantics.
6. Keep observed performance and exercises in Knowledge Validation.
7. Assess coverage per requirement using qualitative evidence dispositions.
8. Do not create a universal candidate score or ranking.
9. Do not include staffing recommendations as source-family records.
10. Define no guaranteed or uncalibrated ramp-time estimate.
11. Preserve planning assumptions separately from capability evidence.
12. Track later outcomes for calibration without assuming causality.
13. Treat missing evidence separately from contradictory evidence.
14. Preserve consent, privacy, correction, accommodation, and fairness context.
15. Report gap coverage and remaining evidence needs, not a hiring verdict.

## Candidate And Successor Evidence Summary

The initial model covers:

1. Evidence-backed organizational requirement references
2. Internal and external candidate subjects
3. Candidate documents and attributed experience claims
4. Scoped corroboration and credentials
5. Links to internal evidence and Knowledge Validation
6. Per-requirement gap-coverage assessments
7. Availability and development-plan assumptions
8. Historical successor outcomes

The governing boundary remains:

> Candidate evidence establishes what was claimed, corroborated, demonstrated, or
> remains unknown for a specific organizational requirement. It supports transparent
> gap-coverage analysis, not automatic ranking, guaranteed readiness, or employment
> decisions.
